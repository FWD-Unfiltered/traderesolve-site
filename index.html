Questions

Who are the Master Assessors?

Experienced tradespeople, many retired or semi-retired, with long, provable careers in their trade. They're impartial and independent of both parties, and judge the work against what was agreed and recognised trade standards.

Do we have to accept the Master Assessor's findings?

You both agree at the start to follow the process. If a dispute still can't be settled, we prepare a TradeResolve Evidence Document, an unbiased and factual record of the agreement, both parties' conduct and all relevant communications, for use in a small claims application or other legal proceedings.

What if my tradesperson won't use it?

That's up to you. Many customers ask for it before choosing who to hire. A tradesperson who's confident in their work has nothing to lose by agreeing.

Can I get my money back if the work is poor?

TradeResolve doesn't hold or refund money. What you get is an expert, impartial view of the work against what you agreed, guidance on putting it right, and, if needed, an Evidence Document to support a small claims application.

Will the Master Assessor just side with the customer?

No. We’re here for both of you equally. The Master Assessor is an experienced tradesperson who judges the work against what you both agreed and recognised trade standards, and both of you get the same report.

Do I have to use it on every job?

No. Use it where it helps: bigger jobs, new customers, or anyone who's nervous about having work done.

Is this insurance?

No. TradeResolve doesn't pay out compensation. It gives you a fair process, an expert assessment and the evidence to resolve a dispute.

What if nothing goes wrong?

Then you never hear from us. Most jobs run smoothly, and knowing an expert could be called keeps everyone straight.

Can I add notes and photos as the job goes on?

Yes. Once you're signed in to My account, you and the other party can add notes and photos to the shared job file at any time. Every entry is dated and can't be changed afterwards, so the file stays a true record of the job.

What happens to my data?

We only use it to run your agreement and resolve any dispute, and we never sell it. Job files are kept for 6 years after the job closes, then deleted. See our privacy policy.

Can I keep it on my phone?

Yes. Open TradeResolve in your phone's browser and choose "Add to Home Screen". It opens like an app, straight to your jobs.

    2. What TradeResolve is, and isn't

    TradeResolve provides a shared agreement, a job file and a structured, impartial resolution pathway for building work, including Master Assessor visits and TradeResolve Evidence Documents. We are not an insurer, a court, an ombudsman, a regulated alternative dispute resolution body or a law firm. We do not guarantee the work of any tradesperson and we do not pay compensation. Nothing in these terms affects your statutory rights.

    3. The Unified Agreement

    The Unified Agreement is between the customer and the tradesperson. It records the job they have agreed. TradeResolve is not a party to the building contract itself. By signing, both parties agree to follow the TradeResolve resolution pathway if an issue arises, to allow a Master Assessor reasonable access to inspect the work, and to act in good faith on the Master Assessor's findings, which are TradeResolve's final position on the issue assessed.

    4. The resolution pathway

    1. Guided steps: either party presses Raise an issue in their job file, which sends TradeResolve a full job report. At each step, the other party has 7 days to respond, and you both try to put it right.
    2. Master Assessor visit: if it's unresolved, either party can request a visit. Each visit costs £265, an additional cost on top of the TradeResolve fee, covering a comprehensive on-site assessment of the work and the preparation of a written Findings Report for both parties. It is paid by the party who requests it. If the Master Assessor's findings go against the other party, the other party repays the £265 to the party who paid. We'll aim to arrange a visit within 10 working days of payment.
    3. Findings are final: the Master Assessor's findings are given to both parties at the same time. They are TradeResolve's final position on the issue assessed, and we will not reassess the same issue. This does not affect either party's statutory rights, including going to court.
    4. Evidence Document: if the dispute still can't be settled, we prepare a TradeResolve Evidence Document for either party to use in a small claims application or other proceedings. A court decides what weight to give it.

    5. Fees and payment

    Our fee is a single flat fee per job, based on the agreed job price: £49 for jobs up to £5,000; £99 for £5,001 to £15,000; £179 for £15,001 to £40,000; and £299 for £40,001 to £100,000. Jobs over £100,000 are priced individually. The prices shown are the total you pay. The parties choose who pays the fee when they sign (the customer, the tradesperson, or split 50/50), and it is payable on signing. The agreement becomes active once the fee has been paid in full.

    The fee covers the Unified Agreement, the shared job file (including changes agreed by both parties), the Certificate of Protection and our guidance through the resolution pathway. A Master Assessor visit is an additional cost of £265 per visit, covering a comprehensive on-site assessment and a written Findings Report, paid by the party who requests it. If the Master Assessor's findings go against the other party, the other party must repay the £265 to the party who paid within 14 days. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

    6. Your right to cancel (customers)

    If you're a consumer, you can cancel within 14 days of signing without giving a reason, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.     Complaints procedure

    Last updated: 10 November 2026

    This procedure is for complaints about TradeResolve's own service, for example how we handled your agreement, a Master Assessor's conduct, or how we treated your data. Disputes between a customer and a tradesperson about the building work go through the resolution pathway instead.

    How to complain

    Email complaints@traderesolve.co.uk. Tell us your agreement reference, what happened and what you'd like us to do.

    What happens next

    1. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 3 working days.
    2. Someone not involved in the original matter will look into it.
    3. We'll give you a full written response within 15 working days. If we need longer, we'll explain why and give you a new date.

    If you are still unhappy after our final response, Citizens Advice can explain your options, including the small claims court.     Impartiality policy

    Last updated: 10 November 2026

    Impartiality is the whole point of TradeResolve. This policy explains how we make sure neither the customer nor the tradesperson is ever favoured.

    Our commitments

    • We’re here for both sides equally. Our role is to establish what was agreed and whether the work meets it.
    • Who pays doesn't change the outcome. Who pays our fee is agreed at the start and has no bearing on any finding.
    • Equal access. Both parties see the same job file, receive findings at the same time, and get the same Evidence Document.
    • Evidence first. Findings are based on the Unified Agreement, the job file, what the Master Assessor sees and measures on site, and recognised trade standards.
    • A true record. Job file entries are time-stamped and can't be edited or deleted, so the record stays a fair account of what happened.

    Conflicts of interest

    Before accepting a job, every Master Assessor confirms they have no connection to either party, including family, friendship, past employment, business dealings or competition in the same local trade within the last 5 years. If a conflict comes to light later, the assessor steps aside and another is appointed at no cost.

    Gifts and hospitality

    Master Assessors must not accept gifts, payments or hospitality from either party. Any offer is recorded in the job file.

    Raising a concern

    If you believe a Master Assessor or anyone at TradeResolve hasn't acted impartially, tell us through our complaints procedure. Concerns about impartiality are reviewed by a director who was not involved.
